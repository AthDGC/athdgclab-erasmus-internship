# AthDGC Laboratory, Erasmus+ Internships

Public site and application materials for **research internships at the AthDGC Laboratory**, hosted by the Department of English Language and Literature, Faculty of Philosophy, at the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens (Erasmus code **G ATHINE01**), supervised by Nikolaos Lavidas, Professor of Diachronic Linguistics.

Live site (after Pages is enabled): **https://athdgc.github.io/athdgclab-erasmus-internship/**

## Hosting

The site is fully static: `index.html` plus a `documents/` folder. To publish, drop the contents anywhere:

- **GitHub Pages**: *Settings → Pages*, set Source to `main` / `/ (root)`, save. The site will be served at `https://athdgc.github.io/athdgclab-erasmus-internship/`.
- **University web space / Netlify / any static host**: upload the folder as-is.

All document links in `index.html` are relative, so the structure must be preserved when uploading.

## How to apply

The call is open now, with no deadline. Send one PDF with your studies and background, your ideas, and what you'd like to do to nlavidas@enl.uoa.gr. We reply within 20 days. The Erasmus+ grant is paid by your home university; the laboratory pays no salary or stipend. Once we accept you, the University of Athens runs its own online application (Learning Agreement signed by three parties, ID, health insurance, accident and civil-liability insurance) and confirms the placement with a Letter of Acceptance. See the [Information Brochure](./documents/AthDGC_Info_Brochure.pdf) and the on-site "How to apply" and "Funding" sections.
